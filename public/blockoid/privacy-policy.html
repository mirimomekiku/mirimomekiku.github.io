Blockoid Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Blockoid is a pixel-art block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game handles and why. In short: your game progress stays on your device, we do not ask for or collect personal information, and any ads are served by Google AdMob under Google's own policies.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your phone only:

  • Scores, coins, adventure progress and stars
  • Unlocked and equipped block skins and themes
  • Settings such as sound, music, vibration, language and accessibility options
  • Daily reward progress and counters used to space out ads

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

No accounts, no personal information

Blockoid has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Advertising

Blockoid may show ads provided by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and usage information. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings under Google > Ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing your results

The game can create a picture of your score or level result. It is only shared if you choose to, through your phone's own share menu, with the app you pick.

Children

Blockoid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through github.com/mirimomekiku.
